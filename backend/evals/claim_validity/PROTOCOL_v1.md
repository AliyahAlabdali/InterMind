# Claim & Score Validity study protocol v1.0

This is a separate, prospective, offline human review of the **existing final run's** reports. It does not amend frozen v1.0 `../PROTOCOL.md`, follow-up labels, Evidence Traceability rules, production prompts, scores, or the pilot. Its rubric, sample, extraction code, and SHA-256 lock are frozen before the paid final run. Pilot errors informed the checks below but are not study observations. If the final run is incomplete, this study cannot produce results.

## Distinct questions

1. **Quotation fidelity (existing v1.0):** Does an evidence string report what the candidate said? Its unit and result remain in v1.0.
2. **Claim validity (this study):** Does a report assertion follow from the full candidate answer, including material qualifiers? The primary result is the mean across reports of each report's fraction of `SUPPORTED` reviewed claims. `PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED`, `UNSUPPORTED`, and `UNCLEAR` remain in the denominator; show all four counts. No claim of candidate-population performance.
3. **Score-band defensibility (this study):** Is the recorded target score's *existing* qualitative band defensible from the answer and the stated rationale? Report `DEFENSIBLE`/`NOT_DEFENSIBLE`/`UNCLEAR` counts over sampled targets. This is not numeric score accuracy. Separately check deterministic overall-score arithmetic and recommendation thresholds for all 15 reports.

## Fixed sample and extraction

`SAMPLE_v1.json` preselects the lexicographically lowest SHA-256 target for each of three categories within each of five frozen jobs; the digest input is `job_id + NUL + category + NUL + target`. All three persona answers for each selected target are included: **45 target assessments**, 3 per report. Review report-wide summary sentences and top-level strength/weakness items for **all 15 reports**. No final output, model score, or verdict influenced this selection. The final run must contain exactly the frozen 15 personas and their ten frozen questions/answers each. The offline tool refuses pilot, incomplete, mismatched, or duplicate outputs. It never asks an LLM to extract or label claims.

For selected target assessments, extract each nonempty `question_evaluations[*].strengths` and `weaknesses` item as one reviewable assertion. For each report, extract each nonempty top-level `strengths` and `weaknesses` item and each sentence of `summary` using the fixed `(?<=[.!?])\s+` splitter. Preserve exact text and JSON location. Remove only exact duplicates within the same report after whitespace and case normalization, retaining all source locations. Do not silently split on `and`: it may join one causal proposition. When an item asserts multiple things, the reviewer judges the whole item and uses `PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED` if only part holds. This item-level unit and its limitation must be disclosed. The inventory also retains the complete relevant frozen candidate answer and question; report-wide claims receive all ten question/answer pairs. Evidence snippets may be shown as context but are not new claims in this study.

Claim IDs are hashes of the report ID, JSON location, and exact claim text; target review IDs are hashes of report ID and target identity. A deterministic shuffle hides persona ordering in the claim sheet. The blind claim sheet omits persona tier, automatic grounding decisions, numeric scores, overall recommendation, and AI suggestions. The score sheet is separate and is released only after initial claim judgments are frozen.

## One-human workflow and locks

1. **Stage A:** One human reviews the blind claim sheet using `RUBRIC_v1.md`, records an initial verdict and a note for every non-`SUPPORTED` case, and checks all 45 target score packets for material omissions *after* the claim sheet is locked. Score packets show recorded scores and bands because those are the object of that separate review. The initial claim and score CSVs receive SHA-256 locks. They are never edited after freezing.
2. **Stage B (optional):** ChatGPT and Gemini may provide suggestions for disputed cases. Store each suggestion with assistant name, item ID, verdict, and rationale in separate AI CSVs. They are AI assistance, never independent human reviewers or ground truth. Blank AI files are valid. Do not expose an initial human verdict when requesting an AI suggestion if independence of the suggestion is desired.
3. **Stage C:** The same human records final claim and score verdicts in separate final CSVs. Record whether AI was considered; if any verdict changes from Stage A, a reason is mandatory. Keep initial, AI, and final files separately. Freeze final files and aggregate only after all required items are complete. The human may retain an initial verdict even when AI disagrees.

Do not report inter-rater reliability. Report the share of initial human verdicts changed at adjudication, with the count of AI-assisted items. The final human verdicts are the study reference, labeled as one-reviewer adjudication.

## Score checks and reporting

Target scores are model judgments on a 0–1 evidence scale. Existing bands are `<0.35` insufficient, `0.35–<0.60` limited, `0.60–<0.80` moderate, and `>=0.80` strong. The human asks whether the band is a reasonable reading of the full answer and qualifications, not which exact decimal is correct. `NOT_DEFENSIBLE` requires a material mismatch; reasonable disagreement within or near a band is `DEFENSIBLE` with a note. Report no MAE, RMSE, human ground-truth scores, or numeric score accuracy. Independently recompute each report's overall score from its target scores: mean within category, then 0.4 competency / 0.4 technology / 0.2 task with missing categories renormalized, rounded to four decimals. Check recommendations at 0.85 strong_hire, 0.65 hire, 0.45 consider, otherwise no_hire. A calculation mismatch blocks aggregation.

Compute a proportion of fully supported claims for each of the 15 reports, then average those 15 proportions; also show pooled raw verdict counts, report claim counts, and per-job/per-tier descriptive summaries. For score-band defensibility, use each report's three reviewed targets as a cluster: average per-report defensible fractions and show raw counts. Material omissions are recorded per selected target and reported separately. Exact textual duplicates within a report count once. Multiple distinct claims from the same answer remain distinct but cannot masquerade as independent reports because report averaging limits their weight. The five jobs and authored personas are purposive, not sampled applicants. Do not attach an independent-claim Wilson interval or infer population confidence; report the range across the five jobs and the small-sample limitation.

## Reproducibility and budget

The study lock records hashes of this protocol, rubric, sample, extraction/aggregation code, the existing v1.0 protocol and label locks, the frozen label copy, both frozen persona dataset files, and `PILOT_SNAPSHOT_v1.json`. The tool also verifies every file named in the v1.0 protocol lock. The snapshot lists SHA-256 hashes for all nine original pilot output files; preparation verifies each. Preparation records the final run metadata and report hashes. Review files and their locks preserve the annotation sequence. Re-running extraction from unchanged inputs must reproduce IDs, text, source context, and order. No paid calls are added: the tool reads the existing final reports. Expect 45 target assessments and 15 report summaries; **claim count is unknown until actual reports exist**. Based on the pilot's roughly 3 target claims and 6 report-wide items per report, plan for about 180–250 review rows, not a claimed result or fixed denominator. Budget approximately 8–12 human hours, including score review and adjudication; dense report-wide claims could take longer. A smaller 15-report sample reduces work but produces coarse, benchmark-specific evidence; report every denominator and avoid narrow confidence claims.

If the frozen final run fails or its outputs are unavailable, report no Claim & Score Validity result. Prompt or scoring changes belong to a separately versioned post-baseline comparison, never this study's preparation.
